## 1. Introduction

"Al Raqi Al Iraqi" (the "App") is a free application that provides Islamic ruqyah content, supplications (adhkar), books, contact links, and a ruqyah booking service. The App does not require you to create an account or sign in. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

By using the App, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the App.

## 2. Information We Collect

**We do not collect any personal data on our servers.** Specifically:

- There are no user accounts or sign-ins.
- We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services.
- We do not ask for your name, phone number, or email address inside the App.
- Core content (texts and books) works on your device without an internet connection.

Any information you enter into third-party apps or websites opened from the App (such as WhatsApp or YouTube) is governed by those services' privacy policies, not by this policy.

## 3. Data Stored on Your Device Only

The App stores a few settings locally on your device. This data is never sent to anyone:

- **SharedPreferences:** night-mode preference, developer-mode state, reading progress, and any contact links or phone numbers you edit.
- **Local database (SQLite):** books you add, audio shortcuts, contact links, and booking details you edit inside the developer dashboard.
- **Cover images:** any image you pick from your gallery to use as a book cover is saved locally on your device only.

All of this data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data from system settings.

## 4. Permissions and Why We Request Them

| Permission | Reason |
|---|---|
| `INTERNET` | To open external links (YouTube, WhatsApp, social media) and to load fonts from Google Fonts when needed. |
| `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | To access media files on older Android versions when you pick a book cover image. |
| `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` / `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO` / `ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION` | Required by the image/file picker libraries; used only when you actively choose an image or file. |

These permissions are not used to collect your data, monitor your activity, or share anything with third parties.

The app requests the appropriate permission once, after you accept this policy on first launch and after showing an explanation, and will not ask again. On Android 14 and above the system picker is used without a permanent permission.

## 5. Third-Party Services

- **Google Fonts** (`fonts.googleapis.com` / `fonts.gstatic.com`): used to load fonts (e.g. Amiri) when needed and cache them on your device.
- **External links:** YouTube, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, X, and TikTok. When you tap them they open outside the App and are governed by their own policies.
- We do not use any advertising, analytics, or behavioral tracking services.

## 6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided data, please contact us (note that we do not store data on servers).

## 7. Data Security

Since we do not transmit your data to servers, the only risks are what you choose to share yourself through external links. We recommend not entering sensitive information into external websites.

## 8. Your Rights

You can at any time:

- Delete the App's data via: Settings → Apps → Al Raqi Al Iraqi → Clear data.
- Uninstall the App; all local data will be removed.
- Contact us with questions about this policy or to request clarification.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if the App's practices change. Any update will be published at this URL and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
